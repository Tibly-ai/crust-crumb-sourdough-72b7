Our Three Ingredient Philosophy

Flour, Water, Salt & Time

We rely strictly on the wild microflora of the Pacific Northwest air to raise our loaves. No industrial yeasts, no additives, and no shortcuts.

1

Stoneground Milling

We source single-varietal wheat directly from regional farms, milled cold on stone to retain all the natural oils, germ, and rich nutrients.

2

Natural Culturing

Our living wild ferment starter is fed twice daily. It imparts the signature subtle tang and makes the gluten easily digestible.

3

Patient Shaping

Each dough is portioned and hand-shaped on wood benches, then slowly cold-proofed for 36 hours to build deep caramelized complexity.

4

Hearth Baking

Loaves hit our screaming hot stone deck ovens, blooming rapidly under heavy steam to form an incredibly crisp, blistered mahogany crust.
