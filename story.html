Born from Wild Air

Three simple elements, shaped by patience.

At Crust & Crumb Sourdough, we do not rely on commercial bakeries' speed tricks. Every loaf we bake is raised using our wild heritage starter, born from Portland's own orchard winds and cultivated daily over years of baking.

By combining this living culture with water, organic sea salt, and stone-ground grains, we co-create bread that tastes deeply of the land it came from. It is a slow, methodical return to real nourishment.

Our Method

The 36-Hour Sourdough Journey

Great bread cannot be rushed. Discover the stages that transform raw grains into our signature dark, blistered crusts and tender interiors.

1

Regional Grain Selection

We partner with local dryland farmers in Oregon to source heritage grains. Our wheat rye and spelt are stoneground fresh to preserve essential nutrients and deep earthy sweetness.

2

36-Hour Fermentation

Time does the heavy lifting. A long cold fermentation allows wild yeasts to slowly break down starches, developing rich lactic acid flavors that are highly digestible.

3

High-Heat Deck Baking

We load our loaves directly onto high-temperature stone decks. Trapping heavy steam for the first half of the bake ensures an airy interior crumb and a beautifully crisp, caramelized crust.
